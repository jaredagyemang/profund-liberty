About Us

Helping families reclaim what's rightfully theirs

ProFund Liberty Group LLC is a surplus funds recovery company dedicated to helping former property owners and their heirs recover money they may not know they're owed.

Who We Are

Specialists in surplus funds recovery

Every year, money from foreclosure and tax sales goes unclaimed simply because the people entitled to it never hear about it, or don't know how to navigate the claims process.

We research public records, locate rightful claimants, and guide them through the steps required to recover their funds. Our work is focused on one thing: helping people recover funds that belong to them, honestly and without upfront cost.

Our Mission

Liberty means a fresh start.

Losing a property is hard. Our mission is to help people take back a measure of financial freedom by recovering the money they are owed, so they can move forward with dignity and a fresh start.

The torch and pillar in our logo stand for that promise: lighting the way through a confusing process, built on a foundation of integrity.

Our Values

What we stand for

Integrity

We tell the truth, even when it means a case isn't a fit. No pressure, no false promises.

Transparency

Our fee and process are spelled out in writing before any work begins. You'll always know where your claim stands.

Education

We explain your rights and options in plain English, including your right to file a claim on your own.

Persistence

Claims can take time and paperwork. We stay on top of every follow-up until your case is resolved.
