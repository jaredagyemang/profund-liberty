Questions

Common questions

Is this a scam?

It's smart to ask. Surplus funds are real money that courts and government agencies hold when a foreclosure or tax sale brings in more than what was owed. ProFund Liberty Group LLC is a registered [STATE OF FORMATION] limited liability company. We will never ask you to pay anything upfront, and we will never pressure you to sign.

Before you sign anything, you can verify the funds yourself by contacting the county or court handling the case. We encourage that, and we'll tell you where to look.

How do you get paid?

We work on a contingency fee. That means we are paid a percentage of the funds actually recovered, and only after they are released. There are no upfront fees, no hourly charges, and no out-of-pocket costs to you. The exact percentage is written in your agreement, and some states limit how much a recovery company may charge.

What if no funds are recovered?

Then you owe us nothing. Because we only get paid from funds that are actually recovered, you take on no financial risk by working with us.
