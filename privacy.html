This Privacy Policy explains how ProFund Liberty Group LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit this website or contact us.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: such as your name, email address, phone number, property address, county, state, contact preferences, and any details you include in a form or message.
  • Technical information: such as browser type, device information, and general usage data that may be collected automatically by our hosting provider or analytics tools.
  • Public records: to review a potential claim, we may research publicly available court, county, and property records.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and review whether surplus funds may be available
  • To contact you by your preferred method, including phone calls and text messages if you have consented
  • To prepare and file claims you have authorized us to pursue
  • To operate, maintain, and improve this website
  • To comply with legal obligations

Text messages and calls

If you agree to receive calls or text messages, we will use your phone number only to communicate about your inquiry or claim. Message and data rates may apply. You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. Consent is not a condition of service. We do not sell or share your phone number or text messaging consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate our business (for example, our form processing provider), with courts or agencies as needed to file a claim you authorized, with attorneys we work with on your claim, or when required by law.

Form processing

Forms on this website are processed by a third-party service (Web3Forms), which delivers your submission to us by email. Their handling of data is governed by their own privacy policy.

Data security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the personal information you have provided, or to stop contacting you, by contacting us using the details below. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
